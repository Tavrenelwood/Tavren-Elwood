The Harsh Reality

You Wrote an Incredible Book. So Why Is Nobody Buying It?

Every year, over 4 million new books are published. The average self-published book sells fewer than 250 copies in its entire lifetime. If your book is not backed by a strategic, multi-channel marketing plan, it will become one of those statistics.

Invisible on Amazon

Your book is buried on page 47 of search results where no reader will ever find it. Your keywords are generic, your categories are too broad, your backend search terms are empty, and your book description reads like a back-cover blurb instead of a conversion-optimized sales page. Without proper Amazon SEO, even the greatest book in the world will sit unnoticed while competitors with inferior content outsell you every single day simply because they invested in proper listing optimization.

Launch Day Was a Complete Flop

You spent months or even years pouring your heart into writing your book, but on launch day you got 12 sales, mostly from friends and family members who felt obligated to support you. The momentum died within 48 hours and your book dropped off the charts completely. A successful launch requires a carefully orchestrated sequence of pre-launch buzz, ARC team coordination, pre-order campaigns, promotional partnerships, and sustained post-launch marketing.

Burning Money on Ads With Zero Return

You have tried running Amazon Ads and Facebook Ads on your own, but your ACOS is hovering around 85 percent, your click-through rates are abysmal, and your return on investment is deeply negative. You are spending more than you are earning and you have no idea which keywords are actually converting versus which ones are silently draining your budget without generating a single sale.

No Cohesive Marketing Strategy

You are doing a little bit of everything and none of it is working. You post on social media sporadically, send a newsletter once every three months, joined a blog tour last year that generated zero sales, ran a free promotion that attracted readers who never bought your other books, and you are constantly chasing the latest marketing trend without any underlying strategy or cohesion.

Weak Visual Marketing Presence

Your book cover might be decent, but your overall visual marketing presence is practically nonexistent. You do not have professional 3D book mockups for your website, social media, and advertising. You do not have a cinematic book trailer to capture attention on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram Reels. In a market where readers judge books by their visual presentation in less than two seconds, weak visuals are silently killing your conversions.

No Presence on Goodreads or Book Clubs

Your book is not on a single Goodreads Listopia list. Book clubs have never heard of your name. Your Goodreads shelf count is in the single digits and you have fewer than 10 reviews on the platform. You are completely missing out on the most powerful organic discovery channels in the entire book industry where readers actively browse for their next read.

Here is the honest truth: A great book does not sell itself. It never has and it never will. Your book needs a strategic, multi-channel marketing engine behind it. That is exactly what I build for every single client I work with.

My journey into book marketing began when I witnessed firsthand how incredibly talented authors were struggling to sell their books despite having phenomenal content.
